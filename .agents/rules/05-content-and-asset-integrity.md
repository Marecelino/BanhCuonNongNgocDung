# Content and Asset Integrity

- Use confirmed menu text and prices exactly as supplied. Format VND consistently, e.g. `30.000đ`.
- Use the confirmed brand name `Bánh Cuốn Nóng Ngọc Dung`, opening time range `05:00–10:30`, phone number, and supplied Google Maps link exactly. Do not infer opening days or a street address.
- Do not invent parking, delivery service, ingredients, preparation claims, awards, reviews, ratings, or promotions.
- Mark unknown fields as `TODO: cần chủ quán xác nhận` in plans; do not show TODO labels in production UI.
- Do not use generic stock food photos as if they were photographs of the actual shop or actual dishes. Use the included local placeholder artwork during design/implementation; each placeholder must be clearly identified in code/docs and replaced by owner-approved real photos before production release.
- Obtain permission for customer/staff photos and testimonials.
- Use images/fonts/icons only with appropriate licenses. Record attribution or license where required.
